01 / Getting Started

Overview

SimpleScore is an iOS and web app for recording match scores simply and quickly. From managing scorers and roster/on-field members to sharing results and backing up data, it covers everything you need on the sideline.

📋
Score Recording
Team A vs Team B. Log goals and own goals in real time
👥
Member Management
Manage roster and on-field members on the spot
📤
Result Sharing
Share match results as formatted text in seconds
☁️
Drive Backup
Auto-save to Google Drive. Up to 5 generations retained
▶️
YouTube Integration
Bulk-update titles and descriptions for match videos
🔴
Live Activities
Real-time score on the Lock Screen & Dynamic Island

03 / Recording a Match

Match Setup

Open the Match Setup screen from the Score Input tab and configure the following before kick-off.

FieldDescriptionRequired
Sleep off Prevents the screen from dimming during a match. Toggle at the very top of Match Setup. Automatically disables when you leave the match screen. Optional
Team Names Names for Team A and Team B. Select from My Teams for auto-fill, or choose from a dropdown of the last 3 used names. Required
Half Duration Duration of each half in minutes. Enables the in-match timer when set. Optional
Roster Members Select players called up for the day. Limits scorer candidates to the roster. Reuse from the last 10 roster lists (saved per team). Optional
On-field Members Track who is currently playing. Toggle on/off with a checkbox during the match. On-field players appear at the top of the scorer list. Optional
💡 Roster and On-field can be used independently or together. Neither is required to start recording a match.
04 / Recording a Match

During the Match

Logging a Goal

Tap the goal button to open the scorer selection screen. On-field players appear at the top of the list.

ActionResult
Select a player Records the goal with the player's number and name
Select ? Records as unknown scorer — shown as "?" in shared text
Select player + add ? Marks the scorer as uncertain — shown as "#10 Sato ?" in shared text
Own goal Adds a point to the opposing team

Half Time & Full Time

Tap End of First Half to move to the half-time screen, where you can share the first-half result. After the second half ends, the app navigates to the Result screen.

Extra Time & Penalty Shootout

Extra time (first and second periods) and penalty shootouts can also be recorded. Phase labels are displayed in English.

05 / Recording a Match

Result & Sharing

After the match, review and share results from the Result screen.

ButtonWhat it shares
Quick Share (Recommended) Full match result in one tap — the easiest sharing option
Detailed Result Full match + first half + second half (+ extra time / PK if applicable)
1st Half / 2nd Half Share each half individually
Extra Time Shown only when extra time was played
Quick Edit Correct scorers, names, or timestamps before sharing. See next section.
💡 When you tap any share button, a preview of the text is shown first so you can confirm the content before sending.
06 / Recording a Match

Quick Edit

After a match — or from the history — you can make small corrections before sharing.

What you can edit

CategoryDetails
Goals Change scorer name/number; add or delete goal events (score is recalculated automatically)
Text only Match name, date/time, team names, match duration text, PK score display text

How to edit

Goal events are listed as rows like "1st Half 5' A #10 Sato". Tap any part of the row — phase, time, team, or scorer — to open a small dialog for editing just that field.

⚠️ You cannot directly overwrite the score number. The score is always derived from goal events — add or delete events to change it.

Editing from History

In Score History, select exactly 1 result and tap "Quick Edit Selected Result" to open the edit screen. Selecting 0 or 2+ results will show an error.

07 / Data Management

Match History

All past match results are listed in the Score History tab.

Deleting & Trash

Select results and tap Delete to move them to the trash. The trash holds up to 10 items, and each can be restored with "Undo".

Reusing Roster Lists

When selecting Roster Members in Match Setup, you can pick from your last 10 roster lists (stored per team) to quickly reuse the same lineup.

08 / Data Management

My Teams

Register your teams and player rosters to reuse them across matches.

Profile Name vs. Score Display Name

FieldPurposeExample
Profile Name
name		 Identifies the team in the My Teams list Friends FC (Yamada)
Score Display Name
displayName		 Name shown on the scoreboard and in match records (optional) Friends FC
💡 If Score Display Name is left blank, the Profile Name is used on the scoreboard. Useful when you have two profiles — e.g. "Friends FC (Yamada)" and "Friends FC (Suzuki)" — that should both appear as "Friends FC" during a match.

Export & Import

Team data can be exported as JSON or CSV. Use this to migrate data to a new device or to keep a manual backup.

09 / Data Management

Google Drive Backup

Match records and team data are automatically backed up to Google Drive. Use it to transfer data to a new device or recover from accidental changes.

1
Sign in to Google
Sign in from the auth status area on the home screen. The app attempts a silent sign-in automatically at launch.
2
Automatic Backup
A backup is triggered automatically whenever data changes (debounced to avoid excessive writes).
3
Auto-Restore on Launch
On startup, the app compares the Drive backup with local data. If Drive is newer, it restores automatically.
4
Manual Generation Restore
Open the Drive tab (tab 4) to view backup history. Up to 5 generations are kept — tap any generation to restore it.
💡 Backups are stored in Google Drive's hidden AppData folder and do not count against your regular Drive storage quota.
10 / Integrations

YouTube Integration

Link your match recordings on YouTube and update their metadata directly from the app.

1
Open the YouTube Tab
Tab 3 automatically fetches your channel's video list and playlists.
2
Link Videos to Match Phases
Assign a video to each match phase (1st half, 2nd half, etc.).
3
Select a Playlist (Optional)
Choose a playlist from the dropdown. "Do not add" is the default option.
4
Tap "Sync to YouTube"
Titles and descriptions are updated in bulk. Videos are also added to the selected playlist at the same time.
11 / Integrations

Live Activities (iOS)

On iOS 16.1 or later, the score and elapsed time are displayed in real time on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island during a match.

LocationContent
Lock Screen Score, team names, and remaining / elapsed time
Dynamic Island Compact score and time display
StandBy Screen Score visible while charging in StandBy mode
💡 You can press the Home button mid-match and still check the score on the Lock Screen. On iOS 16.0 or earlier, Live Activities is silently disabled — all other features work normally.
